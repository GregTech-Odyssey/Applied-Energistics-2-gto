# Suspicious Sky Stone

<BlockImage id="suspicious_sky_stone" scale="8" />

Find a few of these blocks in each Certus Quartz vein in the Ancient World.
Use a <ItemLink id="minecraft:brush" /> to uncover one item. Each of these seven finds has the same chance:

- <ItemLink id="calculation_processor_press" />
- <ItemLink id="engineering_processor_press" />
- <ItemLink id="logic_processor_press" />
- <ItemLink id="silicon_press" />
- <ItemLink id="fluix_upgrade_smithing_template" />
- <ItemLink id="certus_quartz_crystal" />
- <ItemLink id="fluix_crystal" />

After brushing, the block becomes <ItemLink id="sky_stone_block" />.
Mining or breaking it drops no loot, even with Silk Touch.
